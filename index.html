THE HILL

You came as a father. The boy is not in the wheels. You told him Fear Not and you bound him anyway.

Three days up. He asked where the lamb was. You said someone else would see to it. No ram in the thicket.

You stacked the wood. You reached for him. No voice arrived in time.

The knife, the cord, the hill, and his name wait in the dark around the wheels. Carry Fear Not into the light. Hold the center until it takes you in his place.

THE BOY

He walked beside you. He carried the wood because you asked. He believed Fear Not because it came from your mouth.

He asked where the lamb was. You did not say the true answer. The hill already knew.

His name is still in the dark above the wheels. Saying it does not return him. Trading yourself might.
